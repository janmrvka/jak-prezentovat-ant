Rules at a glance
01
One label per block. More dilutes. Rotate it: −10°, 20° or 25°.
02
( ) is brand. [ ] is placeholder. Never overlap inside one frame.
03
Italics only on Inter. Aktiv Grotesk Ex never tilts.
04
Green is signature, not background. No body copy in green.
05
Vykání ven, tykání dovnitř. Inside one channel, never mix.
06
Punchy line at end. Refrain, claim, question — never in the middle.
